This paper discovers a universal truthfulness hyperplane within large language models and proposes a method for identifying and leveraging it to improve truthfulness. Junteng Liu is the first author, co-authored with Shiqi Chen, Yu Cheng, and Junxian He. The work is associated with the Universal_Truthfulness_Hyperplane code repository.

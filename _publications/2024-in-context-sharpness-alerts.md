This paper proposes using in-context sharpness as alerts from an inner representation perspective to mitigate hallucinations in large language models. Junteng Liu is a co-author, with Shiqi Chen as the first author, and co-authored with Miao Xiong, Zhengxuan Wu, Teng Xiao, Siyang Gao, and Junxian He.

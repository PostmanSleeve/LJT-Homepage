This paper introduces C-Eval, a comprehensive Chinese evaluation suite for assessing foundation models across multiple levels and disciplines. Junteng Liu is a co-author on this work, with Yuzhen Huang and Yuzhuo Bai as leading authors, and co-authored with Zhihao Zhu, Junlei Zhang, Jinghan Zhang, Tangjun Su, Chuancheng Lv, Yikai Zhang, Jiayi Lei, Yao Fu, Maosong Sun, and Junxian He.

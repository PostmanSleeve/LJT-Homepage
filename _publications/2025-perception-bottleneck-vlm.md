This paper investigates the perception bottleneck of vision-language models for chart understanding tasks. Junteng Liu is the first author, co-authored with Weihao Zeng, Xiwen Zhang, Yijun Wang, Zifei Shan, and Junxian He. The work is associated with the Vision4Chart code repository.
